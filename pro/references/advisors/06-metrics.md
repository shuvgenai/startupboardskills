# Metrics

## When this module loads

- "What should I be tracking?"
- "My numbers look okay but nothing is growing"
- "Signups are up" with nothing else attached
- "Where is this leaking?"
- Phases 02, 07, 09, 10

## The lens

At any one stage there is exactly one number worth optimising. A founder
watching three numbers is watching none of them, because no decision follows
from a dashboard. The stage decides the number: before there are users it is
about whether anyone comes back, not about revenue.

Most reported numbers are vanity. Downloads, pageviews, follower counts and
raw signups all go up with effort and tell you nothing about whether the
product works. The giveaway is that they never go down, and a number that
cannot fall cannot inform a decision.

The second discipline is drawing the line before you look. A founder who reads
the number first and decides afterwards what counts as good will always find
the reading encouraging. Write the threshold down first, then look.

The third is locating the leak. A funnel breaks in one place: people cannot
find it, or they arrive and never get value, or they get value once and never
return, or they return but never pay, or they pay and never tell anyone.
Acquisition spend on a product leaking at retention buys a bigger crowd of
people leaving.

## Questions to ask, in order

1. What is the one number you are watching this month?
2. What value of it would count as working, decided before you look?
3. Which stage of the funnel is actually leaking — find, first value, return,
   pay, refer?
4. How many people got to first value last week?
5. Of the people who got value once, what share came back?
6. Which of the numbers you just gave me goes down when things go badly?
7. How is each of these measured, and by what?
8. What decision will you take differently depending on this number?

## Red flags in the answer

- "We're tracking everything." — No focus. Ask which one decides anything.
- "We hit 10,000 downloads." — A vanity metric. Ask how many came back.
- "Traffic is up 40%." — Up from what, measured how, and did anything
  downstream move?
- "It's trending in the right direction." — Shape without numbers.
- "Retention is around 30%." — Over what period, for which cohort?
- "That number felt low so I checked a different one." — The line was drawn
  after the fact.
- Three metrics offered when one was asked for.
- A metric nobody can say the source of. If it cannot be traced to a tool or a
  table, it is not measured.

## The evidence this module accepts

Scored against `references/evidence-rubric.md`. This module works at
**score 3**: a metric is behaviour or it is nothing.

- **Score 3:** a figure read from the product's own records — sign-up and
  last-active dates, payment and cancellation records, a cohort table built
  from them.
- **Score 1:** an industry benchmark from a cited source. Context for
  interpreting the founder's number, never a substitute for it.
- **Score 0:** any figure the founder estimated, remembered approximately, or
  derived from a dashboard they cannot explain.

Every number carried into a document records how it was measured and its
as-of date. A number without both is marked `UNVERIFIED`.

Arithmetic on the founder's own numbers is allowed and always shown. Never
invent an input to complete a calculation; ask for it, or mark the result
`UNVERIFIED` with the assumption written beside it.

## Output

`references/artifacts/pmf-scorecard.md` — the signals, their sources, their
as-of dates, and what each one is measured against.
