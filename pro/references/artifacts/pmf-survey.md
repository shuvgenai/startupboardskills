# The very-disappointed survey

**Earned at:** Phase 09, which requires live active users. Never run it with
zero active users.

Four questions, sent to **active users only** — never to a waitlist, never to
the whole email list. The definition of active comes from the founder in
Phase 09 step 1.

## The survey

```
1. How would you feel if you could no longer use <product>?
   - Very disappointed
   - Somewhat disappointed
   - Not disappointed

2. What type of person do you think would benefit most from <product>?

3. What is the main benefit you get from <product>?

4. How could we improve <product> for you?
```

Question 1 produces the number. Questions 2 and 3 tell you which segment to
aim at and in whose words. Question 4 tells you what to build for the
somewhat-disappointed group.

Ask for a split by customer type if the founder can produce one.

## Reading it

| Figure | Value | Responses | verified? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Very disappointed, overall | UNVERIFIED | UNVERIFIED | no |
| Very disappointed, <segment> | UNVERIFIED | UNVERIFIED | no |

Bar, as rules of thumb: about **40%** or more very disappointed, from about
**30** or more responses. Under about 30 responses the result is
**directional** and must be labelled as such.

Never fill a cell with an estimate. An unmeasured figure stays `UNVERIFIED`
and the verdict stays short of Yes.

## Where the answers go

The number and its as-of date go into `## Numbers` in
`references/state-file.md`. The verdict goes into
