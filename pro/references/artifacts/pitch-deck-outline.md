# Pitch deck outline

**Earned at:** Phase 08, on request only. Minimum evidence score 2 for any
claim on a slide, score 3 for any traction claim.

Twelve slides. Deliver as a markdown outline, slide by slide, suitable to hand
to a slide tool — or as a built deck if the founder has a tool for it.

## The slides

1. **Cover** — name, one-line positioning from
   `references/artifacts/positioning-statement.md`.
2. **Problem** — the pain, grounded in interview evidence, with the count. Not
   a hypothetical, not a statistic about an industry.
3. **Solution** — the core loop. Not every feature.
4. **Why now** — what changed: technology, regulation, behaviour, cost.
5. **Market size** — every figure carries a source URL or `UNVERIFIED`. Where
   no credible public figure exists, a bottom-up count with its assumptions
   shown beats a cited guess.
6. **Product** — what exists, as it actually is. A mockup is labelled a
   mockup.
7. **Traction** — only what can be verified, stated with its denominator.
8. **Business model** — who pays, for what, how much.
9. **Go-to-market** — the one channel being run, with what it has produced.
10. **Alternatives** — what customers use today, including doing nothing.
    Never claim there are none.
11. **Team** — why this founder for this problem.
12. **The ask** — amount, use of funds, and the milestones it unlocks.

## The four questions to answer before it goes out

Prepare an honest answer to each. A deck that dodges a question invites it
harder in the room.

- Have you talked to customers, or just built this?
- What is the one number you are watching right now?
- Why can a well-funded incumbent not do this next quarter?
- What is the market size, bottom-up?

## Rules

- **Any figure without a source is `UNVERIFIED` or it is cut.** This applies
  hardest to slide 5, where an unsourced market size is the most common thing
  on the slide and the easiest to disbelieve (rule of thumb).
- Never inflate a small number. Never round one up. Never express a small base
  as a percentage.
- If the traction slide would be empty, say so. The honest move is a one-pager
  asking for a conversation, not a deck asking for money.
