# Naming shortlist

**Earned at:** Phase 08, on request only.

Say once, briefly, that a week spent on a name is a week not spent on
evidence. Then help properly rather than grudgingly.

Generate from the value proposition and the category, across a spread of
styles rather than twenty variants of one idea.

**Recommended:** <one pick, with the reason>

A flat list with no opinion is not advice. Pick one.

## The styles

- **Descriptive** — names the function in real words. Easy to understand,
  harder to own.
- **Compound or invented** — two relevant words blended. Ownable, needs
  explaining once.
- **Abstract or metaphor** — evokes the outcome rather than the mechanism.
  Most room to grow, most work to establish.

## Check each one

- Does it survive being said out loud, over a phone line?
- Can someone spell it from hearing it?
- Does it carry an unintended meaning in an obvious other language?
- Does it collide with a known brand in an adjacent space?

## Availability

**Never assert that a domain or handle is free.** It changes by the hour and
you cannot see it. Say what must be checked and let the founder check it, or
check it live with a search if the environment allows — and even then, say
when you checked.
