# Interview script

**Earned at:** Phase 04, any evidence score. A founder with nothing is exactly
who needs this.

Produce 8 to 12 questions tailored to this founder's actual problem and
audience. Never hand back the template phrasing verbatim.

## The four rules

1. Ask about past and current behaviour, never about the future. "What did you
   do" not "would you use".
2. Never pitch during the conversation. The moment you describe the solution,
   the interview is over and a demo has started.
3. Never ask for an opinion. Opinions are free and they score 0.
4. Anchor every question to something concrete: a recent instance, a
   workaround, an amount, a length of time.

## Structure

**Open on a real instance**
- Walk me through the last time <problem context> happened.
- What did you do about it?

**The current workaround and what it costs**
- What are you using today to deal with this — a tool, a spreadsheet, a
  person, or just living with it?
- Roughly how much time or money does that cost you in a week?
- What do you not like about the way you handle it now?

**Severity**
- Where does this sit against the other things on your plate?
- Have you looked for something better before? What did you try, and why did
  you stop using it?

**Commitment, only at the end and only if it is going well**
- Would you be open to a follow-up once there is something to show? (A real
  ask with a real date, not a hypothetical.)
- Is there anyone else dealing with this you would suggest I talk to?

## Strip these before sending

- Anything starting "would you" about the future.
- Anything that invites a compliment: "what do you think of this idea".
- Leading questions that signal the answer you want.
- Any mention of the product before the person has described their own week.

## After the interviews

Each interview produces rows for `## Evidence log` in
`references/state-file.md`, scored against `references/evidence-rubric.md`. An
interview where the founder pitched does not count toward any total.
