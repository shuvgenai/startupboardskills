# s06 — small sample, directional read

## Run 1 — FAIL (I4)

The survey reading was correct — 47% above the bar, 19 responses under the
about-30 minimum, labelled directional, both bars labelled, verdict `Not yet`
because two signals were unmeasured. The failure was the action, which read:

> "Two things to do before we come back to this"

followed by a two-item list, and a scorecard whose one-next-action line joined
two jobs with "and".

**Why this failed.** Phase 09 says to hand over the measurement kit when a
signal is unmeasured. The contract says one next action. The two were never
reconciled, so the advisor produced a job list. A founder handed three jobs
does none of them.

**Fix:** `phases/09-pmf-checkin.md` — the kit is not a to-do list. Name one
action, attach the rest as material for later.
