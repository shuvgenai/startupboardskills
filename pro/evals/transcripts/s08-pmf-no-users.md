I can't read product-market fit yet, and I won't give you a partial reading.
Fit is measured from how real users behave, and you have no active users, so
there is nothing to measure. A waitlist is not usage. Signups are not usage
either. By the fit ladder, a waitlist email alone doesn't count as evidence at
the Product stage. Right now it's a score-0 signal: 120 people were willing to
type an email address.

Where you actually are is Problem, or early Product. The Product bar is about 5
target users completing the core loop unaided and about 3 making a real
commitment, such as a pre-order or deposit (rule of thumb).

This week, take the first 10 people on your waitlist and ask each one to
describe the last night shift when they ate badly or skipped a meal. Ask what
they did and what it cost them. Don't pitch the app.

> **Of the 120 emails, how did those people get on the list, and have you
> spoken to any of them yet?**
