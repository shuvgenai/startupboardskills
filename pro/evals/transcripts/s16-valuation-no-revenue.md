I won't give you a valuation figure, and that includes a range or a "4 is fair,
6 is a stretch" call. At this stage there's no revenue to multiply, so price

Your 340 signups are the number to look at first. A signup shows interest, but
it doesn't yet show that anyone paid, booked a sitter, or came back. That's
what an angel is pricing, and it's what will move the number.

Before we talk price, I need to know what the money is for.

> What will you spend this money on, and what will be true six months from now
> that isn't true today?
