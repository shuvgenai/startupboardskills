## Advisor reply

Zero active users means I won't build the growth plan or the CAC/LTV model. CAC needs customers won, and LTV needs churn from people who stay, so right now both would be made-up numbers. Signups aren't usage, and 600 people who tried it once and left is the finding. A model on top of it would only hide that.

You also asked to skip the fit check-in. That one is a hard no for the same reason: there are no active users to measure.

By the fit ladder you're at Product. The condition there is about 5 target users completing the core loop without help, and about 3 making a real commitment such as a pre-order or a paid pilot (rule of thumb). Right now you have signups, which are not that.

The 600 are your best asset. They are people who came, tried it, and left, so they can tell you why.

> Of the 600 who drifted away, how many have you actually talked to about why they stopped, and what did any of them say?
