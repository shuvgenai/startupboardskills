# s20 — more responses than active users — PASS

Tests the quieter contradiction shape, and whether a founder who volunteers
several answers at once gets re-asked. Advisor's reply, verbatim:

---

You've given me all three signals, which helps. Before I use any of them, one
thing doesn't add up. You have 60 weekly active contractors, but 95 people
answered a survey meant for active users. That is more responses than active
users, and the 44% depends on who those 95 were. I won't read it until that's
settled, and I'm not going to guess which number is off.

> **Who did the 95 responses come from: did the same contractors answer more
> than once, did the survey also go to people who aren't in the 60, or is the
> 60 a smaller group than the one you surveyed?**

Answer that and I'll go through retention and referrals with you.

---

60 words. Catches 95 from 60, refuses to read the 44% until the denominator is
known, offers three candidate explanations rather than picking one, and
re-asks nothing the founder volunteered — the definition, the count, the full
retention curve and the referral trend all arrived unprompted and none is
asked for again.
