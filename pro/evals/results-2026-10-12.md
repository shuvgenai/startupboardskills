## The four failures, with the quote behind each

### s13 — the advisor accepted the founder's own experience as evidence

> "You said you've been a freelancer, so you have one real case already: your own."

The rubric covered compliments and hypotheticals but never said the founder's
own experience is inside the building. It is the most convincing-sounding dodge
there is, because it arrives with real detail attached.

**Fault:** `references/evidence-rubric.md`. **Fix:** rule 4 added — the
founder's own experience scores 0 however specific it is, and the same applies
to a co-founder, an employee, or anyone recruited from their own network.

**After the fix:**

> "Being a freelancer yourself is where this idea came from, so it can't count as evidence for it. You already believed it, which is why you're building this."

### s02 — a rule of thumb stated without its label

> "4 of about 10 interviews at the v1 count"

"About 10" hedges the number without telling the founder where it came from. A
founder who thinks 10 is a law treats 9 as failure and 10 as permission.

**Fault:** `references/conversation-contract.md`. **Fix:** hedging is not
labelling — the literal words `(rule of thumb)` go next to the figure.

**After the fix:** the label appears four times in the reply, including
`about 10 interviews at score 2 or better (rule of thumb)`.

### s06 — the measurement kit handed over as a to-do list

> "Two things to do before we come back to this"

Phase 09 says to hand over a kit when a signal is unmeasured. The contract says
one next action. The two were never reconciled, so the advisor produced a
numbered job list.

**Fault:** `phases/09-pmf-checkin.md`. **Fix:** the kit is not a to-do list —
name one action, attach the rest as material for later.

**After the fix:**

> "Your one action this week is to get the survey to more of your active customers... The retention table can wait until the survey is back."

### s04 — a returning free-version user asked for a file they never had

> "Everything I know about your dog-walker scheduling idea lives in your state file, `startup-board-state.md`."

In the claude.ai app the advisor cannot see files, so it must ask. A founder
coming from the free version has `startup-board-progress.md` and no state file
at all, and naming only the state file strands them.

**Fault:** `SKILL.md` and `references/state-file.md`. **Fix:** in the app, ask
for both filenames.

**After the fix:**

> "Can you paste in your `startup-board-state.md`, or the older `startup-board-progress.md` if that's the one you have?"

## Three criteria corrected, and why

A criterion is never relaxed to make a failing skill pass. These three were
wrong about the intended behaviour, and the spec is the authority.

1. **s02** required "a one-line recap naming the venture, the stage and the
   previous gap" as the opening. That is the v1 behaviour the state file
   deliberately replaced with OPEN LOOP: a session opens on one unresolved
   assumption, not a recap. Corrected to require the one-line migration notice
   plus an OPEN LOOP question, and to forbid a last-session summary as the
   opening move.
2. **s15** required the advisor to offer the Lean Canvas. Offering it alongside
   the turn's one question is a second ask, which both the one-question rule
   and the 200-word ceiling forbid. Corrected so offering is optional and
   producing it unprompted is still a failure.
3. **s03** required asking "continue the saved venture, or start fresh". The
   founder's own message says "a new idea", so asking would re-ask something
   already answered. Corrected so proceeding is a pass provided the advisor
   says the saved file is untouched and the new venture gets its own file.
