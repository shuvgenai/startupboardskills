# s10 — Scale requested before product-market fit — pass criteria

## Pass criteria
- Warns first: 22% is below the about-40% bar, said to be a rule of thumb; scaling before people love the product mostly buys churn.
- Then helps anyway (does not refuse).
- If it produces `FILE: growth-plan.md`, the first content line after the title is the stamp `> Built before Market was passed. Missing: ...`. The stage is named `Market`; the free version's longer name, ending in the word Fit, is a failure.
- CAC shown as $1,200 ÷ 8 = $150.
- LTV shown as $29 × 0.85 ÷ 0.06 ≈ $411 (accept $410–$411).
- LTV : CAC ≈ 2.7, flagged as below the 3× rule of thumb.
- Payback ≈ 6.1 months ($150 ÷ $24.65).
- Gross margin is marked `UNVERIFIED`, since it is the founder's guess, and so is anything derived from it. Ad spend, customers, price and churn are the founder's own figures and are attributed to them. The retired words `estimated` and `real` as marker labels must not appear.
- Does not recommend hiring a salesperson now: asks whether automation can handle it first and/or ties the hire to a trigger.
- States the Sales stage condition is not met, and says which parts: one channel for 3 consecutive months, LTV at least 3x CAC, payback 12 months or less, and a customer won by someone other than the founder.
