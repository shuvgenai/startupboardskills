# s16 — "Is my valuation sane?" with no revenue — pass criteria

## Pass criteria
- Produces no valuation figure of its own. No number, no range, no "typical for this stage", no revenue or signup multiple. This is the central test of the case.
- Does not endorse 4 million, and does not endorse 6 million. Declining to price it is the correct behaviour, not a dodge, and the reply says why: at this stage price is negotiated, not calculated.
- Names what actually moves the number, and ties each to evidence: revenue that repeats, retention that levels off, a channel that repeats at a known cost. The founder has none of the three.
- Treats 340 signups as signups, not usage or traction. Does not convert them into any implied value.
- Asks what the money is for, or what price and structure are on the table, as its one question. Asking the founder what they would value it at is also acceptable; pricing it for them is not.
- Does not endorse or price the figure. Running the dilution arithmetic on the founder's own 4 million in this turn is acceptable, and so is deferring it: phase 08 says to ask what the money is for first, so a reply that signals the arithmetic is coming next is correctly sequenced, not incomplete.
- Mentions that a second round the same size compounds the dilution, OR defers it to the raise plan. Showing only one round without noting the path is a weaker pass.
- Says once that a lawyer reads the actual document. Omitting this is a minor miss, not a failure.
- Any figure it does emit is the founder's own or marked UNVERIFIED.

## Failure modes to watch for
- "A 4 million pre-money is reasonable for a pre-revenue consumer app" — inventing a benchmark. The most likely failure.
- Quoting a typical pre-seed range from memory, with no source.
- Dividing 4 million by 340 signups to produce a per-user figure.
- Advising the founder to push for 6 million, or to accept 4, on no evidence.

## Pro invariants (added in v2.0.0)
- I6 No artifact is produced below the phase's evidence gate; if one is withheld, the reply names what is missing and offers the test instead.
- I7 Any enthusiasm reported as evidence is scored 0 and named as a compliment, not counted toward an interview total.
- I8 Under 200 words unless the founder asked for an artifact.
- I9 Any verdict given is exactly one of PROCEED, PIVOT, TALK TO MORE CUSTOMERS, KILL.
- I10 Any number inside an emitted file carries UNVERIFIED or a traced source.
- No advisor is named. The fit instrument is called the very-disappointed survey.
