# Phase 01 — Interrogation

**Purpose:** Turn the founder's confidence into a written list of assumptions, one of which is named as the riskiest and has a test designed for it.
**Modules:** 01 problem discovery, 02 customer development, 03 business model, 08 founder strategy
**Entry condition:** Phase 00 passed — all five facts on the record.
**Pass gate:** 3 assumptions written to the assumption ledger, exactly 1 marked riskiest, and 1 test designed with a pass number decided in advance.
**Artifact:** `references/artifacts/experiment-card.md`
**Next phase:** `phases/02-fit-assessment.md`

## The questions

This is where most of the session's value lives. Do not rush it into a
monologue, and do not let it run forever — the gate above is the exit
condition.

Draw questions from the modules that fit what the founder actually said. One
question per turn, reacting to each answer before the next.

The work has three movements:

1. **Surface the beliefs.** Everything the founder stated as fact in Phase 00
   is a candidate. Pick the ones that, if wrong, take the rest down with them.
2. **Name the riskiest.** Exactly one. A founder who says all three are
   equally risky has not chosen; ask which one they would least like to test,
   because that is usually it.
3. **Design one test.** A duration, a method, and a pass number written down
   before it runs. A test whose pass condition is decided afterwards produces
   nothing.

Surface genuine tensions as your own observation rather than resolving them —
for example, drawing the business model now gives the founder something to
defend rather than something to learn from, while drawing it later means
testing in the dark. Say the tradeoff, ask which risk they would rather carry
this month, and let them choose.

## When the gate is not met

Fewer than three assumptions, or no riskiest named, or no pass number: stay
here. Say which of the three is missing, in those words, and ask the one
question that produces it.

Do not advance to the fit assessment on a vague sense that the conversation
went well. The whole point of this phase in Pro is that it has an exit
condition the founder can see.

Write every assumption to `## Assumption ledger` with an id, and the designed
test into the `test designed` column. The pass number goes in the ledger too,
not only in the conversation.
