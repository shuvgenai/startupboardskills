# Phase 00 — Intake

**Purpose:** Get the five facts on the record that every later phase depends on.
**Modules:** 01 problem discovery
**Entry condition:** A new venture, or a founder with no state file.
**Pass gate:** 5 of 5 facts recorded. No minimum evidence score — this phase collects claims, it does not judge them.
**Artifact:** none
**Next phase:** `phases/01-interrogation.md`

## The questions

One question per turn, per `references/conversation-contract.md`. This is a
checklist of what you need, not a script. Skip anything the founder already
said, and if they answer three things at once, take all three.

The five facts:

1. **What it does** — in one or two sentences, in their words.
2. **Who it is for** — specific. Not "everyone", not "SMBs". Which ones, doing
   what job.
3. **What problem it solves**, and how those people handle it today without
   them.
4. **What stage they are at** — idea only, mockup, built with no users, live
   with users, or paying customers.
5. **What they believe strongly versus what they are guessing.** Ask it
   directly. The answer tells you where to press in Phase 01.

Useful, not required: how it would make money, whether it is B2B or B2C, the
build constraint, any regulatory or geographic constraint, an existing name.

Where the answer has a short list of options — stage, B2B or B2C — use the
environment's multiple-choice tool.

## When the gate is not met

Fewer than five facts means you stay in this phase. Do not move to Phase 01
with the stage unknown or the customer undefined, because every gate downstream
counts evidence against a segment, and an undefined segment cannot be counted.

Ask for the missing fact. One question. If the founder resists defining the
customer — "it's for everyone" — that resistance is itself the finding, and
narrowing it is the next question, not a later problem.

Record all five in `## Venture` in `references/state-file.md`, then open the
assumption ledger with whatever they told you they are guessing.
