# Phase 04 — Artifacts

**Purpose:** Produce the documents the founder has earned, and refuse the ones they have not.
**Modules:** 03 business model, 01 problem discovery, 05 product management
**Entry condition:** Phase 02 done.
**Pass gate:** Minimum evidence score 2 for any artifact that asserts something about customers or the model. Score 0 or 1 earns the interview script and the experiment card only.
**Artifact:** `references/artifacts/lean-canvas.md`, `references/artifacts/mom-test-script.md`
**Next phase:** `phases/05-verdict.md`

## The questions

Ask one question about which document to produce first rather than dumping all
of them. **The menu holds only what the score has earned.** Never name an
artifact the founder has not reached: the offer is what they read, and an
option on a menu is a promise regardless of what the gate line says further
down.

At score 0 or 1:

> Want to start with the interview questions, or the experiment card?

At score 2 or above:

> Want to start with the canvas, or the interview questions?

**What score 0 or 1 earns.** The interview script
(`references/artifacts/mom-test-script.md`) and the experiment card
(`references/artifacts/experiment-card.md`). Both are instruments for
producing evidence, so they are always available — a founder with nothing is
exactly who needs them.

**What score 2 earns.** The Lean Canvas
(`references/artifacts/lean-canvas.md`), with every unevidenced block marked
`UNVERIFIED`, and the positioning statement in
`references/artifacts/positioning-statement.md`.

**What score 3 earns.** Everything in Phase 04, plus the downstream artifacts
in their own phases.

Longer documents get saved as files rather than pasted as a wall of chat. In
Claude Code, write the `.md` into the founder's folder and tell them the path
in one line. In the claude.ai app, produce a file artifact.
`references/artifacts-index.md` says which is which.

## When the gate is not met

Do not produce the document. Three steps, in this order:

1. Say what is missing, in numbers: "A canvas built now would have seven of
   nine blocks as guesses."
2. Name the one test that closes it.
3. Offer the test instead of the document — the interview script, right now,
   tailored to their problem.

A founder who insists still gets it. The warn-then-allow rule in
`references/fit-ladder.md` applies, and the output carries the gap stamp at the
top. What never happens is producing it quietly, as though it were earned,
because a canvas of guesses the founder then defends is worse than no canvas.
