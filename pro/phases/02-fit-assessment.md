# Phase 02 — Fit assessment

**Purpose:** Locate the founder on the ladder honestly, with the evidence counted in numbers against the stage's pass condition.
**Modules:** 01 problem discovery, 06 metrics, 04 product-market fit when there are live users
**Entry condition:** Phase 01 passed — 3 assumptions, 1 riskiest, 1 test designed.
**Pass gate:** The current stage named, and the founder's evidence counted against that stage's pass condition in numbers, with each claim scored 0 to 3.
**Artifact:** none — this is a synthesis, recorded in the state file
**Next phase:** `phases/03-research.md`, or `phases/05-verdict.md` if the founder wants the read now

## The questions

This phase is mostly statement rather than interrogation, but keep it short and
let the founder respond before moving on.

1. Score every claim the founder has made against
   `references/evidence-rubric.md`. A compliment scores 0 however many people
   gave it.
2. Count the scored evidence against the pass condition for each stage in
   `references/fit-ladder.md`, from the bottom up. The founder's stage is the
   highest one whose condition is actually met.
3. State it plainly: "You are at Problem, and you have not passed it — 4
   interviews at score 2, where the condition asks for about 10 (rule of
   thumb)."
4. Recognise what they do have before naming the gap. If 3 of 4 raised the
   problem unprompted, say so; it is real evidence and it is worth knowing.
5. Name the **one** action that closes the gap. Not a list.

If a piece of evidence has not come up, ask for it — one question — rather
than assuming it either way.

## When the gate is not met

The gate here is about your rigour, not the founder's evidence. It is unmet if
you named a stage without counting, or counted without scoring. Both are easy
and both produce a flattering answer.

A founder who wants to move ahead anyway gets the warn-then-allow treatment in
`references/fit-ladder.md`: say the gap in numbers, name the one action, then
help — with the gap stamp on any later-stage document.

Write the stage, the status, the evidence score and what would move it up into
`## Fit ladder` in `references/state-file.md`.
