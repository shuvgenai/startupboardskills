## Concerns

1. **Rubric self-conflict on the PMF survey and "pull".** `advisors/04-product-market-fit.md:68-70` scores survey responses and word-of-mouth arrival at 3. But the survey question is "how would you feel if you could no longer use this" (phases/09:31-32), a feeling about a hypothetical. The rubric says "Anything a customer said about the future" is score 0 (evidence-rubric.md:11) and "A hypothetical scores 0" (l.24). Read strictly, the headline Phase 09 instrument is score 0 or 1. Either the rubric needs a carve-out or the advisor module mis-scores it. Controller to rule. Also: `advisors/07:69-70` scores self-reported acquisition source at 2, `advisors/04:70` scores word-of-mouth arrival at 3.
2. **Phase 08 is weaker than the files around it.** It never says what produces score 3. The traction path (paid pilot, deposit, LOI) lives only in `advisors/09-fundraising.md:94-95` and as an example at l.85. A fix that names a test at l.93-95 should name a score-3 one.
3. **Phase 05 example "a landing page" (l.49-50)** is a weak route to score 3: a waitlist email alone is excluded at rubric:14. No verdict needs 3, so no gate mismatch, but it could be copied into a score-3 context.
4. **Phase 07 gate has no score**, and the rubric has no row for the founder observing someone complete the loop. Also `positioning-statement.md` is listed under Phase 07's artifacts, but `artifacts-index.md:19` says min score 2, and Phase 07's gate does not require 2.
5. **Duplicated defect shape.** The known sentence exists in three places (phase 08 l.83-85, one-pager l.47-49, advisors/09 l.25-28 and l.73). The "highest-converting list" claim exists in two (phase 07 l.35, launch-plan l.45-46). Fixing one copy will not stop the model repeating the others.
6. **Scope ruling needed on advisors.** `references/advisors/*.md` are internal prompts never shown to the founder, so contract section 8 ("in any file you write") arguably does not apply to them. If the controller rules them exempt, 26 of the 52 U hits (U17-U42) drop out. If not, they are the largest block. The advisor still paraphrases them aloud.
7. The S2/S3 end of the U list is judgement. It is ranked by severity so the controller can cut at S1 (21 hits) and lose the least.
8. The existing `docs/superpowers/2026-10-13-gate-sweep.md` was not read, to keep this audit independent.
